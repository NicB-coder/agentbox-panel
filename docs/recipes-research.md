# Best recipes: research notes

Nic's request was just "do research on best recipes", so I took it to mean popular, well-reviewed dinner recipes. Nothing in this project mentions recipes, so I made no code changes.

There is no single official "best recipe" ranking. Each site has its own list, so the best way to pick is to see which dishes show up on several lists.

## Lists worth opening
- Allrecipes, 55 best dinners of all time, based on thousands of 5-star reviews: https://www.aol.com/articles/55-best-dinner-recipes-time-002433727.html
- Simply Recipes, 34 best dinners of all time: https://www.aol.com/articles/34-best-dinner-recipes-time-193000695.html
- Allrecipes, 20 most-saved recipes of 2026 so far: https://www.aol.com/20-most-popular-recipes-2026-180000844.html
- Food Network, top-rated and reviewed: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/photos/top-rated-and-reviewed-recipes.html
- America's Test Kitchen, most-saved of 2025: https://www.americastestkitchen.com/collections/the-most-saved-recipes-of-2025
- The Modern Proper, 60 easy dinners: https://themodernproper.com/60-best-easy-dinners

## Dishes that keep coming up
- Lasagna (one Allrecipes version has 19,000+ ratings)
- Chicken pot pie
- Shepherd's pie
- Beef stroganoff
- Honey garlic chicken thighs (a 2026 favorite)
- Grilled shrimp
- Bolognese

## Caveats
This came from two quick web searches, and I read only the search summaries, not the full pages. Counts such as "19,000 ratings" come from those summaries and I didn't check them. If Nic wants something narrower (desserts, vegetarian, cheap student meals), say so and I can dig deeper.
